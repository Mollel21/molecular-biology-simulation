DNA EXTRACTION

DNA / RNA Extraction

Extraction and purification of DNA or RNA from cells or tissue samples using a virtual molecular biology laboratory.

Intermediate ⏱ 20–30 minutes 🧪 Virtual Laboratory

🎯 Aim

To extract and purify nucleic acids from biological samples for downstream molecular biology applications.

🔬 Principle

The extraction process involves cell lysis, removal of contaminants, nucleic acid precipitation or binding, washing, and elution to obtain purified nucleic acid.

📋 Objectives

  • Understand the major stages involved in nucleic acid extraction.
  • Identify the equipment and materials used during extraction.
  • Perform the major extraction steps in a virtual laboratory.
  • Understand the importance of obtaining purified nucleic acid.

🔧 Equipment

  • Micropipette
  • Microcentrifuge
  • Microcentrifuge tubes
  • Vortex mixer
  • Heating/incubation equipment

🧪 Materials

  • Biological sample
  • Lysis buffer
  • Proteinase K
  • Washing solution
  • Elution solution

📝 Procedure

1
Prepare the biological sample

Prepare the sample for the nucleic acid extraction process.

2
Perform cell lysis

Break open the cells to release nucleic acids.

3
Remove cellular contaminants

Remove unwanted cellular components from the sample.

4
Bind or precipitate nucleic acid

Separate the nucleic acid from other components of the sample.

5
Wash the nucleic acid

Remove remaining contaminants from the nucleic acid.

6
Elute the purified nucleic acid

Collect the purified nucleic acid ready for downstream applications.
